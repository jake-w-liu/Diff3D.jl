Geometry

Buffer geometry, primitive generators, curves, paths and shapes, extrusion, polyhedra, CSG, and geometry utilities.

Diff3D.BoxGeometry — Method
BoxGeometry(; width=1, height=1, depth=1, width_segments=1, height_segments=1,
            depth_segments=1)

Axis-aligned box centred on the origin (three.js BoxGeometry). Each side is a separate grid of vertices with flat normals, built in three.js's order (+x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z) with its vertex layout, UVs and triangles, so indices and raycast face_index values match three.js. three.js's per-side draw groups are not added.

Diff3D.compute_tangents! — Method
compute_tangents!(geo)

Compute per-vertex tangents from positions, normals, and UVs and store them as the 4-component :tangent attribute (x, y, z, handedness), following three.js BufferGeometry.computeTangents. Each face adds its UV-aligned s and t directions to its three vertices; a face whose UV determinant has no finite reciprocal is skipped. Each vertex tangent is then Gram-Schmidt orthogonalized against its normal and normalized (a zero result stays zero), and the handedness is -1 when cross(normal, tangent) points against the summed t direction, else 1. Only the faces of the draw groups are visited when geo has groups; vertices no visited face references keep the tangent they already had (zero for a newly created attribute).

The geometry must be indexed and carry a normal and a UV for every vertex; otherwise an ArgumentError is thrown. An existing Float64 tangent attribute with item size 4 and one entry per vertex is updated in place; any other :tangent attribute is replaced. Returns geo.

Diff3D.merge_geometries — Method
merge_geometries(geos; with_groups=true)

Merge multiple BufferGeometry objects into one (for batching). The merged positions, normals, UVs, indices, and carried-over named attributes contain each input's logical n_vertices/n_faces payload; surplus backing-array elements are ignored. Normals are retained only when every nonempty input supplies a complete logical normal buffer; otherwise the merged normals are left empty for downstream recomputation. UVs follow the same all-or-empty rule. When with_groups=true (default), a draw group is appended per input geometry recording which faces came from which sub-mesh: (start, count, material_index) with 1-based start, count equal to that input's face count, and material_index equal to its 0-based position in geos. Inputs that contribute no faces are skipped so empty groups are not emitted.

source
Diff3D.merge_groups! — Method
merge_groups!(geometry) -> geometry

Reorder the faces so that draw groups with the same material index are contiguous, then merge them (three.js BufferGeometryUtils.mergeGroups). Groups are sorted by material index and then start, the faces of each group are concatenated in that order (faces outside every group are dropped, faces in overlapping groups repeat), and adjacent groups with the same material become one. A geometry without groups is returned unchanged. Groups outside the faces raise an ArgumentError.

Morph-target attributes are carried along from the first merged vertex but do not participate in the comparison. Faces, draw groups, and the draw range are preserved; normals and UVs are kept only when they cover every vertex.

Diff3D.to_creased_normals — Method
to_creased_normals(geometry; crease_angle=π/3) -> BufferGeometry

Return an unindexed copy of geometry (see to_non_indexed) with normals that are smooth across edges whose faces meet at less than crease_angle radians and creased elsewhere (three.js BufferGeometryUtils.toCreasedNormals). Corners at the same position (to two decimal places, truncated as three.js does) share an averaging set; each corner's normal is the normalized sum of the unit normals of the faces around its position whose dot product with its own face normal exceeds cos(crease_angle).

Diff3D.to_non_indexed — Method
to_non_indexed(geometry) -> BufferGeometry

Return a copy of geometry in which every index entry has its own vertex (three.js BufferGeometry.toNonIndexed). Positions, normals, UVs and every named attribute (morph targets included) are gathered through the index, and draw groups are copied; as in three.js the draw range is not. Triangle geometry keeps one face per three consecutive vertices, with indices 1:3n_faces; for unindexed geometry (empty indices) the geometry itself is returned, as three.js returns this. Line geometry with an index becomes unindexed line geometry.

Normals, UVs and named attributes that do not cover n_vertices raise an ArgumentError.

Diff3D.to_triangles_draw_mode! — Method
to_triangles_draw_mode!(geometry, mode::Symbol) -> geometry

Convert triangle-strip or triangle-fan topology to a triangle list (three.js BufferGeometryUtils.toTrianglesDrawMode). The strip or fan order is geometry.indices, or the vertex order 1:n_vertices when there are no indices; mode is :triangle_strip or :triangle_fan, and :triangles returns the geometry unchanged. A strip alternates the winding of odd triangles as WebGL does, and a fan pivots on its first entry. The result has count - 2 faces and no draw groups. Fewer than three entries or an unknown mode raise an ArgumentError.

Diff3D.Curve — Type
Curve{P}

Abstract parametric curve with points of type P (Vec2{Float64} or Vec3{Float64}), the counterpart of three.js Curve. Every curve supports get_point, get_point_at, get_points, get_spaced_points, get_length, get_lengths, get_u_to_t_mapping, get_tangent and get_tangent_at; 3D curves also support compute_frenet_frames and TubeGeometry. t is the curve parameter and u ∈ [0, 1] the fraction of arc length, approximated as in three.js from 200 chords.

Implementations: LineCurve, LineCurve3, QuadraticBezierCurve, QuadraticBezierCurve3, CubicBezierCurve, CubicBezierCurve3, EllipseCurve (and ArcCurve), SplineCurve, CatmullRomCurve, and the composite CurvePath, Path and Shape.

Diff3D.DecalGeometry — Method
DecalGeometry(mesh::Mesh, position::Vec3, orientation::Euler, size::Vec3)

Decal geometry projected onto mesh (three.js addon DecalGeometry). A box projector of extent size, centred at the world-space position and rotated by orientation, cuts the mesh's triangles (in world space, through the mesh's world matrix) to the box. The result is an unindexed world-space triangle geometry with UVs 0.5 .+ (x, y) ./ (size.x, size.y) in projector space; it carries the mesh's normals, transformed by its normal matrix and linearly interpolated at the cut (not renormalized, as in three.js), when the mesh geometry has normals. Use it with a mesh at the origin, e.g. Mesh(DecalGeometry(target, position, orientation, size), decal_material).

The geometry's base positions are used (morph targets and skinning are not applied, as in three.js). size must have finite non-zero components.

Diff3D.ParametricGeometry — Function
ParametricGeometry(fn, slices=8, stacks=8)

Triangle geometry sampled from fn(u, v) -> Vec3 on a regular grid of u = j / slices, v = i / stacks (three.js ParametricGeometry, including its defaults). Rows of constant v are stored in order of u; the UV of each vertex is (u, v), and each grid cell is split into the triangles (a, b, d) and (b, c, d) with a = (u, v), b = (u + du, v), c = (u + du, v + dv), d = (u, v + dv). Normals are cross(∂p/∂u, ∂p/∂v) normalized, with the tangents approximated by one-sided differences of step 1e-5 (backward where u - 1e-5 >= 0, else forward; likewise for v), so they are continuous across seams of closed surfaces. A zero cross product gives a zero normal; where the differences overflow or underflow, the same direction is formed with rescaling (three.js would produce NaN or zero there).

fn must return a Vec3 with finite coordinates at every sample, including the difference samples, or an ArgumentError is thrown. See ParametricFunctions for three.js's example surfaces.

Diff3D.RoundedBoxGeometry — Method
RoundedBoxGeometry(; width=1, height=1, depth=1, segments=2, radius=0.1)

Box with rounded edges and corners (three.js addon RoundedBoxGeometry). The radius is limited to half the shortest side, and each rounded edge has segments subdivisions. As in three.js the result is built from a unit BoxGeometry with 2segments + 1 subdivisions per side, made unindexed (indices 1:n_vertices), each vertex is pushed onto the rounded surface with the surface normal, and the UVs run around each side's arcs. With segments = 0 three.js returns that unit box unchanged (ignoring width, height, depth and radius), and so does this constructor.

The dimensions must be finite and positive and the radius finite and non-negative (a zero side would make three.js's UVs NaN).

Diff3D.TubeGeometry — Method

Diff3D.edges_geometry — Method
edges_geometry(geo; threshold_angle=deg2rad(1))

Boundary edges and the edges between faces whose normals differ by at least threshold_angle radians, as an unindexed line geometry (three.js EdgesGeometry, whose default threshold is 1 degree). This follows EdgesGeometry.js exactly:

  • corners are welded by position rounded to four decimal places (Math.round(coordinate * 1e4)), and triangles with two welded corners are skipped;
  • each directed edge a→b is recorded once; when a later triangle supplies the reverse edge b→a, the pair is consumed and emitted, in that later triangle's direction, if the normals' dot product is at most cos(threshold_angle);
  • the edges left unpaired (boundaries, and the extra sides of non-manifold edges) follow, in the order they were first recorded.

Diff3D.to_shapes — Method
to_shapes(shape_path::ShapePath) -> Vector{Shape}

Sort the sub-paths into Shapes with holes (three.js ShapePath.toShapes). Sub-paths with fewer than three points (at 12 divisions) or zero area are dropped. Working from the largest area down, each sub-path is nested in the smallest larger sub-path whose bounding box contains its box and whose outline contains its interior point; the winding numbers then decide under fill_rule whether it is filled. A filled sub-path inside a hole or at the top level becomes an outline, any other filled one a hole of its container. The shapes share the sub-paths' curves.

source
Diff3D.wireframe_geometry — Method
wireframe_geometry(geo)

All unique triangle edges as line segments (three.js WireframeGeometry). Faces are visited in index order, or group by group when geo has draw groups (faces outside every group are skipped). Each triangle (a, b, c) contributes its edges a→b, b→c, c→a in that direction, and an edge is emitted only the first time its two end positions occur in either direction, so triangles that share an edge through separate but coincident vertices contribute it once.

Returns an unindexed line geometry (n_faces = 0) whose consecutive vertex pairs are the segments. Throws an ArgumentError when a draw group lies outside the geometry's faces.

Diff3D.apply_matrix4! — Method
apply_matrix4!(geometry, matrix) -> geometry

Transform geometry in place by the 4×4 matrix (three.js BufferGeometry.applyMatrix4): positions are transformed as points (with the perspective divide), normals by the normal matrix (the inverse transpose of the upper-left 3×3 block; zero when it is singular) and renormalized, and the x, y, z of a :tangent attribute as directions and renormalized, keeping its handedness w. As in three.js the triangle winding is kept, so a mirroring matrix turns the faces inside out (use transform_geometry to keep them front-facing).

Unlike three.js, which leaves morphAttributes untouched, morphPosition<k> deltas are transformed by the matrix's linear part and morphNormal<k> / morphTangent<k> deltas so that each morphed direction is the transformed, renormalized base direction plus the delta; this keeps morph targets attached to the moved geometry, and it needs an affine matrix. Bounds are not cached on a geometry, so later compute_bounding_box and compute_bounding_sphere calls see the new positions.

The geometry is validated before anything changes; an ArgumentError leaves it unmodified.
